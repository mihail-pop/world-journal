# Introduction

## My Note

Welcome to the documentation and thank you for checking out World Journal.

I wanted one place where I could explore what is happening around the world, keep the sources visible and write down the things I did not want to forget. I could not find an app that handled those parts together in the way I wanted, so I built this one.

World Journal is intentionally local and personal. It does not need an account, a paid API or a separate frontend service. Your journal and settings stay on the machine running the app, while news articles and videos still open at their original sources.

I use the app myself and plan to keep improving it. It is not trying to become a large hosted platform. It is simply meant to be useful, understandable and dependable for people who want this particular kind of research space.
