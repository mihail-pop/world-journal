How sources work

Choices save as you change them. Turning off a publisher pauses its feeds and retains their individual choices. Feeds refresh while Django runs, even with the browser closed.

Article feeds match country references in headlines and summaries. Videos match country references in titles. Custom sources can contribute to any country; adding a feed does not assign every item to every country. Playback starts only after Play.
