What is included

The ZIP includes entries, collections, subsections, country tags, local images, country favorites, map presets, overview notes, and news-source preferences. Original dates and video timestamps are retained.

Feed caches and browser preferences such as theme, home country, and viewport are excluded. Save open editors before exporting. If an image is missing, export will report it instead of creating an incomplete backup.
