About these figures

Figures follow Wikidata's nondeprecated statements, using preferred rank and then the newest supplied date. Capitals, languages and currencies use rank within each territorial scope. Equally ranked conflicts are kept visible. Area follows the source's reported definition, can include water and may differ from the drawn map.
