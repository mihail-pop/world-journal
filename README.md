World Journal is the local, self-hosted app I wanted for following world news and keeping my research in one place. It combines an interactive world map, attributed news feeds and a personal journal without requiring accounts, API keys or a cloud service.

> **Note:** World Journal is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0. It is free software and its source must remain available when modified versions are offered over a network.

## Features

- Explore 238 countries and 14 custom territories on Simple, Terrain and Adventure maps.
- Follow country and world news from Google News, regional RSS feeds and public YouTube feeds.
- Keep Markdown notes, article references, video timestamps, country tags and local images in one journal.
- Organize entries into collections and search or filter them by country, date and type.
- Save favorite countries and create named map color presets.
- Browse a rolling 90-day news archive without downloading article bodies or videos.
- Keep one movable video player running while navigating through the app.
- Export journal data, preferences and local attachments in a portable backup.
